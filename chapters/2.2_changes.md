## Points to confirm before this goes to the committee

1. **Paper 7, "13 MENA countries".** The paper says "13 MENA countries" in its contribution section. Please confirm the final sample size, because a reviewer will count it.
2. **Paper 10 vs Paper 11, innovation measure.** Both use green-technology patents. You describe Paper 11 as "green patents", which is right, and Paper 10 the same way, also right, but a reader may not see why the two papers differ. The narrative section 2.5.4 already handles this; the table does not need to.
3. **Definitions.** "Load capacity factor", "NARDL", "MMQR", "ecological footprint" and the rest are not spelled out in the running text of the section, by design: the table cells stay short and the notes carry the abbreviations. If the physics-check is that section 2.2 must define first uses on its own, tell me and I will add a sentence.
4. **Paper 5 estimator.** The paper prints "moment quantile regression (MACS)" in its abbreviation list, which is a typo for method of moments quantile regression (MMQR). The table follows your draft and uses MMQR.
5. **Table 2.2, Paper 12 and Paper 10.** Both are described in the literature as "green patents"; Paper 12's own label is "green technologies". Your draft already reflects this difference and I have left it.
